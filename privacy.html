TimeMachineGarage Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how TimeMachineGarage handles information when users authorize supported social media integrations, including TikTok.

1. Information We Process

When a user connects a TikTok account, TimeMachineGarage may process information provided through TikTok's official APIs, such as account identifiers, authorization information, access tokens, and information required to publish authorized content.

2. How Information Is Used

Information is used only as necessary to provide requested functionality, including authenticating an authorized TikTok account and publishing short-form automotive video content to that account.

3. TikTok Authorization

TimeMachineGarage only accesses TikTok account functionality after the user has explicitly authorized the application through TikTok.

Users may revoke authorization through their TikTok account settings or other controls provided by TikTok.

4. Data Storage and Security

Authorization credentials and related technical information may be stored securely when required for the operation of the service. Reasonable technical measures are used to protect this information from unauthorized access or disclosure.

5. Sharing of Information

TimeMachineGarage does not sell personal information. Information is not shared with third parties except when necessary to operate the requested service, comply with applicable law, or interact with services explicitly authorized by the user.

6. Content and Publishing

Videos, captions, and related content are submitted to TikTok only when authorized through the connected account and the TimeMachineGarage publishing workflow.

7. Data Retention

Information is retained only for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the relevant functionality, maintain the integration, or satisfy applicable legal or security requirements.

8. User Rights and Deletion Requests

Users may revoke TikTok access through TikTok and may request deletion of information associated with their authorization where applicable.

9. Third-Party Services

TimeMachineGarage may use third-party services such as TikTok and hosting or automation platforms. Their handling of information is governed by their own privacy policies and terms.

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when functionality, platform requirements, or applicable regulations change. The latest version will be published on this page.

11. Contact

Questions regarding this Privacy Policy may be directed to TimeMachineGarage through its official social media channels.
